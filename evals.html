## 4. Limits, and what I'd test next

AI design critique is not product validation. ChatGPT and DeepSeek reviewed the plan and the app in 9 rounds (score 84 → 93). That sharpened the thinking but proves nothing about real users.

  • These checks prove the rules are right, not that families or venues will use the product.
  • Venues, prices and bookings are sample data; payments are simulated.
Next question How I'd test it Success looks like
Can first-time users book without help? 5 people, one-line task, timed, no demo 5 of 5 finish in under a minute
Will owners keep the calendar live? 2-week pilot with 5 venues in one area 4 of 5 log every offline booking within a day
Will families pay 10% before visiting? 10% vs a flat ₹5,000 advance Payment rate up, cancellations not up
