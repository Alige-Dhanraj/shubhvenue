ShubhVenue — Prompts

I built ShubhVenue with Claude and used ChatGPT and DeepSeek as critics. The prompts below are the record of 11 product calls I made, including 1 version I killed myself and 4 pieces of AI advice I overruled or reframed. Evals are on a separate page.

1. Where I overruled the AI

The reviewers advised; I decided. These are the calls where I went against them, and why.

AI advice My call Why
Switch to "request to book": a wedding hall isn't a cinema seat Rejected The date + hall is the scarce, standard part. Waiting is the exact problem I set out to remove. Menu and decor are agreed after the date is safe.
Show families "calendar updated X hours ago" for trust Reframed A timestamp says "not real-time". Families see a "Live" badge; venues that don't confirm their calendar lose it and rank lower.
Drop business events to stay focused Kept 3, filtered They only show suitable venues, and I name them as the first thing I'd cut.
Remove "sample data" labels so it feels real Kept, made subtle Unlabelled fake data would cost more trust than it gains.
Add interview numbers it suggested Rejected I didn't collect them, so they don't go in.
Add a WhatsApp intake for owners Accepted It fits how venues already take bookings, which is my biggest adoption risk.

2. The 5 decisions that shaped the product

Decision I started with What was wrong The prompt I wrote Trade-off I accepted
Instant booking, not request-and-wait Families "request" a date; the venue confirms It was the same phone call with extra steps "If availability is live, why would a family request? Let them book instantly with a small advance and pay the rest later, like a movie ticket." Only works if the calendar is truly live, so trust becomes the owner's job
Availability per hall, not per venue One "free / booked" per venue One venue often has 2–3 halls; one booked hall hid the others "Study how venue software works for owners. Keep only what a prototype needs, and say why." More data for owners to keep updated
Real-time means real-time "Calendar updated 16 hours ago" on every card It told families the data might be stale "Families should see live availability, like a cinema seat. Keeping the calendar honest is the owner's job." Needs an owner nudge and a ranking penalty to stay true
Show the full cost, not hall rent Cards showed hall rent only Food, GST and deposit surprise families later "Map both journeys, find where each step breaks, and build only features that fix a break." Totals look bigger than competitors' hall-only prices
Reviews now, verified reviews later Reviews ranked last in my priority table Families won't book a venue they can't trust "Don't you think reviews are crucial for any venue? Re-score them." Imported Google ratings first; only real bookings can leave reviews later

3. The rest of the build, in four phases

  1. Problem. "The problem isn't finding venues, it's that no one has reliable availability. Design for both sides." → a two-sided product. "Tell my real story and back it with secondary research; check every number." → sourced market data, unverified claims removed.
  2. Plan before code. "Don't build yet. Have the plan judged by a strict reviewer; build only after it approves." → 3 plan revisions before any code.
  3. Build. "Make it usable for a month: many venues, receipts, coupons, payouts. Learn from BookMyShow, Zomato, Swiggy and OYO." → 40 venues, bookings Nov 2026–Mar 2027. "Use real Bangalore areas and Indian events, including business events." → 10 areas, 12 event types.
  4. Simplify and check. "Rewrite the reviewer page so a 15-year-old understands it." → 10 short sections. Every version re-tested with the automated checks before publishing.
